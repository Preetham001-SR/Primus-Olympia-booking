Senior living just got its most luxurious address in Bangalore, and we are sharing it with a selected few!

Embrace your retirement life with pride and passion at Primus Olympia, located within Gopalan Olympia township. These ready-to-move-in 2 BHK senior assisted living apartments are crafted to elevate every moment of senior living. Here, luxury concierge services, bespoke healthcare, and deeply nurtured family bonds come together in a community that celebrates life. Set in the heart of Kumbalgodu, these homes truly embody Primus' renowned Blue Zone philosophy, inspiring a richer, fuller, and more fulfilling way to live. With only 112 active senior living apartments, this rare opportunity to experience elevated senior living is not something you want to miss!

Primus Olympia offers senior citizen independent living apartments within Gopalan Olympia's thriving community off Mysore Road, Kumbalgodu, blending luxury senior living with independence and a classic bouquet of Primus services.

You can find 2 BHK apartments starting from ₹98L at Primus Olympia.

This community is perfect for those seeking active senior living apartments that offer privacy, luxury, and community joys in a warm and supportive environment.

Primus adds luxury assisted services like concierge, on-site healthcare, senior-friendly designs, and vibrant amenities, defining what senior assisted living apartments truly mean at Primus Olympia.

Gopalan Enterprises is a trusted Bangalore developer of premium projects like the 16-acre Gopalan Olympia (awarded Budget Apartment of the Year 2022), delivering ready homes with vast clubhouses and sports facilities, making Primus Olympia one of the most developer-backed senior living communities in Bangalore.

Primus Senior Living, pioneering in luxury assisted living since 2013, starting in Bangalore and expanding to Chennai, Pune, and beyond, is renowned for creating the finest senior citizen independent living apartments with concierge, nutrition, and healthcare services.

Residents of our senior assisted living apartments benefit from 24/7 medical care, an on-campus clinic, nursing staff, physiotherapy, specialist consultations, preventive healthcare via partners, and a dedicated emergency response system.

These active senior living apartments at Primus Olympia come with 30+ luxury amenities, including common facilities like a multipurpose hall, an Olympic-size pool, a gym, a game room, an art gallery, and entertainment centres, along with senior living amenities like a medical centre, restaurant, concierge, housekeeping, and preventive healthcare.

Contact Primus Olympia for site visits, bookings, and other details.
